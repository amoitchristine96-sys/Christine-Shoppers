Our Story

Christine Shoppers started with a simple goal: make online shopping in Uganda easy, affordable, and trustworthy. From fridges and TVs to jeans, t-shirts and cosmetics — we curate products families actually need.

We partner with reliable suppliers, offer secure payments, and deliver across Kampala and nationwide. Every order is backed by friendly customer support.

Our mission: To provide quality products at fair prices with exceptional service to every Ugandan household.
